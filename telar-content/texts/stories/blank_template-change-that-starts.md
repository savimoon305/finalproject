Spoken words galvanize the moment, whereas written words create and enduring structural testament. Rodriquez innovated the manner in which speech could be presented. Demonstrating the need for a new way of governing, a new way to educate, a new way to think, he was defiant to create a whole new system in Latin America. He belived that his innovation with typeface to recreate the gestures that were missing, would lead the people to understand, feel, and be moved by his ideas; and inturn lead to a people united through belief and a more powerful Republic. Language is the primary vehicle for human consciousnsess empathy and collective action.
