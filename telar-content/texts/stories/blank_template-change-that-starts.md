Language is the primary vehicle for human consciousnsess, empathy and collective action. Spoken words galvanize the moment, whereas written words create an enduring structural testament. Rodriguez innovated the manner in which speech could be presented. In _Luce y Virtues Sociales_, he demonstrates a new way of governing, a new way to educate, a new way to think, he was driven to create a whole new system in Latin America. He believed that his innovation with typeface to recreate the gestures of speech that were missing in print, would lead the people to understand, feel, and be moved by his ideas; and inturn lead to a people united through belief and a more powerful Republic.    ![simon rodriguez](https://savimoon305.github.io/finalproject/iiif/objects/simonrodriguez/simonrodriguez.jpg)
