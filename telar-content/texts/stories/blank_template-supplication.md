---
title: "Supplication "
---

In 1615, Guaman Poma de Ayala supplicated the King of Spain for change and to create a new scocio-political order. His Nueva Corónica y Bien Gobierno was a 1,189 page letter written to the King of Spain, by using the colonizer's Spanish, and the Christian theology,  to outline the mis-treatment of the indigenous and his bold plans for the reconstruction of the governance of Peru. It wa imperative that Guman Poma pose as a noble to get his message documented, as it was a unique point of veiw coming from an Andean. Guaman Poma accompanied his writing with 398 of his drawings depicting Andean as the 'true pious Christians'. He not only used his writing to serve as a historical chronicle, an instructional sermon, but also as a legal petition to the King for political change.
