---
title: "Supplication "
---

In 1615, Guaman Poma de Ayala supplicated the King of Spain to instill a new scocio-political oreder that merges Inca economic structure with Christian theology by outlining the suffering and unfair treatment of the native people. His Nueva Corónica y Bien Gobierno was a 1,189 page letter written to the King of Spain, coming from the rare Andean viewpoint, to outline the mis-treatment of the indigenous and his bold plans for the reconstruction of Peru. Guamn Poma accompanied his writing with 398 of his drawings depicting Andean as the 'true pious Christians'. He not only used his writing to serve as a historical chronicle, an instructional sermon, but also as a legal petition to the King for political change.
