title: "Valades's image and its connection to writing and speech"
---

The friars literally holding up the church describes the centralized goal of Christianizing New Spain. This new influx of religion uses both writing and speech to communicate what is wanted in a Christian society. Surrounding the church are pictorials of noblemen, possibly like Valades, who are speaking to groups of people.

This included institutions that are presented all over the page, such as education. Education was used to ensure the public was literate: reading, writing, and speech. This subject will be further analyzed in later steps. 

However, this literacy was assumed among all the people of Spain who are expected to read and understand it. Alphabetic literacy was a major stepping stone for Valades to overcome when wanting to spread his Christian message. Essentially, Valades's ability to read, and other educators around him give a gateway for a chance for common folk to write and speech.
