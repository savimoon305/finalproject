title: "A Connection between Writing and Speech "
---

Even though a Khipu is not a traditional mode of writing (e.g., not with letters and words), it is still a form of communication and record-keeping. 

In Elizabeth Hill Boone's article, "Writing and Recording Knowledge," she elaborates on how different systems of writing, such as a quipu, don't have to follow a certain rhetoric for speech. For example, she notes that "...the quipu, is not known to have any phonetic component." (Boone 18). This sets quipus apart as more of a visual device rather than a device that connects writing and speech.

However, as said on the previous panel, The quipukamayoc is the one to put a phonetic meaning to the knots, differnt colors, placement, etc.
