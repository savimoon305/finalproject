Looking at the quipu and at the mixtec codex, no meaning is conveyed through speech directly, however these artifacts were certainly connected to speech within their time. Those who could read quipus (the _quipucamayocs_) had to eventually relay the information contained in the quipus, an action which would certainly require speech. And the mixtec codex originally held many stories, which were no doubt discussed by the people of the culture they were a part of, which also involves speech.
