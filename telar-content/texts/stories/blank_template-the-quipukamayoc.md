The khipukamayoc is an individual who can translate khipus.
Below, is a drawing by Guaman Poma from his book called the "Nueva Crónica y buen gobierno." It displays a quipukamayoc (Right) translating and reciting information to a noble man.
![](https://juancobo.com/138la/iiif/poma/p338/manifest.json)

To further analyze the quipu; there are nine knots that are identified and clustered together. Whether they were on the same piece of cord or the amount of knots in one loop. However, this is where we are limited. We are limited where we can only read the number of units by the knot and placement, as done in class while making our own khipu's, the activity was to be able to identify parts of the khipu and understand how to read one. However, we still don't know WHAT the knots translate too. For example with the image above, we don't know what the quipukamayoc is telling the nobleman. Is it the smount of maize? The amount of tribute from another location? What the quipukamayoc owns? It is something that is still unknown because of the loss of the translator.

Even though a Khipu is not a traditional mode of writing (e., not with letters and words), it still is a form of communication with record-keeping.
